## Reopening rule

The total-sum-only subfamily is retired because it has a concrete exact shortfall, not
because a full-space upper bound has been proved.  The universal Proposition-3 route
may be reopened only with a proof that every Type I sequence in its stated class has
the missing Siegel--Walfisz property, or with a replacement equidistribution theorem.
The specialized Heath--Brown route avoids that class, and the `A=1/4` route is
logically independent of both repairs.
