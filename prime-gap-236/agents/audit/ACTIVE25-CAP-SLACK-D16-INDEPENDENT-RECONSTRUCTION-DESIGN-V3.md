# Active-25 cap-slack/D16 independent reconstruction design v3

Status: design only. No target integration is authorized or reported here.

The staged producer and its assembler are only a conditional serialization
route. Before any combined quotient or theorem claim, a new checker must
reconstruct the arithmetic without importing either staged program and without
using their serialized rational values as arithmetic inputs.

## Frozen inputs

The checker must independently pin and parse the reviewed v2 planner tuple,
its independent checker/result/report tuple, the one-face v3 gate tuple, the
cap-shell D2 exact form, the fixed radial D16 certificate, and every transitive
geometry/integrator input named by those files. Duplicate JSON keys,
noncanonical rational strings, nonfinite numbers, symlinks, hard links, and
byte/hash mismatches are fatal.

## Independent arithmetic route

For every common count `r=0..25` and every
`h=0..floor(eta/delta)-r`, the checker must:

1. Rebuild the four distinguished-coordinate cap marginals directly from
   Definition 1. For degrees 1 and 2 it must use literal endpoint
   antiderivatives, not call or copy the producer's marginal routine.
2. Rebuild the fixed radial D16 marginal from the certificate basis and
   vector. It may reuse the independently audited exact integration library,
   but it must not consume a staged polynomial, domain, or integral.
3. Lift each radial orbit through the angular density, canonicalize the exact
   rational domain, and accumulate the signed `RH-RL` and `VH-VL` combination.
   A second deliberately ungrouped implementation must agree on a declared
   sample containing all four marginal branches and both positive degrees.
4. Require support only on labels of total count `r` or `r+1`, and sum all
   faces to one exact 38-coordinate cross vector.

The checker then independently parses the frozen D2 cap-shell sparse `I` and
`48J` forms and restricts them to the canonical pilot labels: degree zero for
all counts and degrees 1 and 2 only for counts 9 through 14. It constructs a
39-dimensional pencil whose first coordinate is the fixed radial D16
coordinate. The off-diagonal entry is exactly
`48 * J(inner,C_(R,d))`; there is no extra polarization factor in the matrix
entry. Both exact matrices must agree byte-for-byte after canonical rational
serialization with a fresh assembly from the independently reconstructed
cross vector.

## Dynamic record audit

The checker must receive the ledger and completed-manifest SHA/device/inode
bindings from outside the attempt directory. It must reopen and rebind the
authorization, ledger, all 26 common-count stages, and manifest; verify the
one-shot leaf set and distinct singly linked regular inodes; and reject any
prefix, resumed attempt, replaced path, duplicate key, or noncanonical JSON.
Serialized stages are comparison evidence only.

## Claim boundary

The reconstruction output must retain
`independent_arithmetic_reconstruction=false` until every step above has run
successfully in the new checker. Numerical eigendiscovery, if later added, may
select a rational vector only. Any published denominator, numerator, margin,
or quotient must be freshly contracted from exact rational matrices and that
vector, and remains non-theorem evidence until the wider analytic certificate
is separately checked.
